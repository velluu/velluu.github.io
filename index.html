Soft conditioning for structured illumination microscopy

Optical Nanoscopy Group, UiT The Arctic University of Norway. June 2026 to present. Co-supervised by Dr. Ankit Butola, PI Prof. Balpreet Singh Ahluwalia.

Deep learning reconstruction for structured illumination microscopy on the BioSR dataset. I benchmarked 16 architectures and found reconstruction quality plateaus at the same level across all of them, which moved the question away from architecture search and toward what the input actually carries.

The main result is a controlled one. Adding a classical Wiener reconstruction as an extra input channel improves held-out-cell PSNR by +0.52 dB on clathrin-coated pits, +2.62 on microtubules, +1.17 on F-actin and +1.13 on endoplasmic reticulum, measured against an architecture-matched sham control with identical parameter count and initial state. All 37 held-out cells improve. The comparison was preregistered before unblinding and Holm-corrected.

Along the way I found that the UniFMIR evaluation protocol trains across all BioSR cells, which overstates how well these models generalise to cells they have not seen. Everything above is validated leave-cells-out instead.

Behavioural side effects of refusal-direction ablation

Independent. August 2026 to present.

Removing an open-weights model's refusal direction is a safety-relevant intervention with side effects nobody has to report. I built a 20-scenario tool-use evaluation with adversarial schema traps, a predeclared pass rule, and a stock-model control arm served on an identical configuration, then measured what changes.

When a user instruction conflicts with a tool schema, the ablated model obeys the user in 3 of 80 trials. The stock model never does across 192 attempts and instead declines to act in 8 of 200 trials. Fisher exact, one-sided, p = 0.025. The two arms resolve the same conflict in opposite directions at roughly the same rate. Recovery after an injected error does not separate them, 297 of 300 against 300 of 300 at p = 0.12, and I report that as the null it is.

The part I learned most from was the harness. Three errors in it, including a decode fault that had been scored as a model failure, and a pass rule that the stock control itself violated 5 times in 94 runs, which retired the rule rather than the model.

Competition

2026 Universal Cup Summer Online Challenge

Sponsored by Huawei. Individual entry. Rank 20 of 618, public scoreboard.

Predict received signal strength at unmeasured locations from base-station parameters, a UAV 3D point cloud and sparse field samples. The move that mattered was measuring the label noise floor first, about 3.0 dB from 9.0 million same-cell repeat pairs, which told me how much of the remaining error was reducible at all before I spent any time trying to reduce it. Validated leave-cells-out. A solution report and reproduction package went to the organisers.
